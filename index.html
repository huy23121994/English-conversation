Core toolkit — dùng mọi buổi

Công thức kéo dài câu trả lời:

Answer→Reason→Example→Extra info

Tần suất:

  • I usually / normally + V
  • I tend to + V — tôi có xu hướng...
  • once or twice a week, almost every day

Đưa opinion:

  • I think... / I don't think...
  • In my opinion, / Personally, I think...
  • Thêm tương phản: However, ...

Không đồng ý (lịch sự):

  • I see your point, but...
  • I understand what you mean, but...
  • I'm not sure I agree with that.

Hỏi ngược lại:

  • What about you? / How about you?
  • Do you feel the same way?

Follow-up chain mẫu: How often? → Who with? → Why do you like it? → Since when?

Câu "cứu mạng"

Cần thời gian suy nghĩ:

  • Let me think.
  • That's a good question.
  • I've never really thought about that.

Quên từ:

  • I don't know the exact word, but it's something like...
  • It's kind of like... / How can I say this in English?

Không hiểu người kia:

  • Could you say that again?
  • Could you speak a little more slowly?
  • What do you mean by...?
